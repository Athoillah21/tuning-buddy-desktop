Watch the 5-part guide

Paste a slow PostgreSQL query. Tuning Buddy runs it with EXPLAIN ANALYZE, asks the AI provider you choose for index and rewrite ideas, tests each idea on a temporary copy of your tables, and keeps only the ones that are measurably faster. You get a report with the bottleneck and the before-and-after timings. Your rows stay on your PC: the AI sees the query and its plan, never the data. Also inside: a database browser, a test-data generator and 33 benchmark cases.
